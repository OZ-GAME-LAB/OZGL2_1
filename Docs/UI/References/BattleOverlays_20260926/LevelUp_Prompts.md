# 레벨업 오버레이 레퍼런스 — 2026-09-26

- 생성 방식: built-in image_gen, 각 안 개별 생성.
- 피그마 기준: https://www.figma.com/design/Kz3TNcpjEhLXT7lGoCWinf?node-id=95-300
- 구조: 3택1, 마름모 아이콘, 증강 이름/설명/보너스 스탯/현재 레벨.
- 스타일 참고: ../BattleHUD_20260923/04_Unified_GothicCommandPanel_v3.png
- 공격·방어·마력 강화 및 10%/레벨 0은 디자인 비교용 예시이며 실제 게임 데이터로 적용하지 않았습니다.
- 새 버튼이나 저장·증강 적용 기능을 구현하지 않았습니다. Unity와 Figma 원본은 수정하지 않았습니다.
- 이미지 4종을 각 1회 시각 확인: 세 선택, 이름·설명·수치, 프레임 간격과 배경 대비 확인. 03은 마법진/제단의 연출 비중이 큽니다.

## 01_GothicMetal

출력: `LevelUp_01_GothicMetal.png`

```text
Use case: ui-mockup.
Create ONE polished full-screen 16:9 Korean dark-fantasy pixel-art game LEVEL-UP AUGMENT SELECTION OVERLAY reference, not a collage or contact sheet.
Input image 1 is the Figma functional wireframe: preserve its THREE evenly spaced choices with a diamond icon above each, title, description, bonus stat and current level. It is NOT a palette reference. Input image 2 is the game's actual visual identity AND background: retain a recognisable dim battle field and its top HUD behind a strong translucent black overlay. Darkened background must never compete with the choices. Match the intentional crisp pixel clusters, ivory segmented metal, deep oxblood crimson, obsidian and noble wine velvet; sophisticated aristocratic gothic game interface, not dirt/rough stone. Front-facing flat orthographic screen mockup, precisely aligned UI.
Core foreground text: top title "레벨 업", smaller "증강 하나를 선택하세요". Three choices left to right:
1 "공격 강화", white pixel sword inside crimson diamond, description "아군의 공격력이 증가합니다.", bonus "공격력 +10%", footer "현재 레벨 0".
2 "방어 강화", white pixel shield inside antique-gold diamond, description "아군의 방어력이 증가합니다.", bonus "방어력 +10%", footer "현재 레벨 0".
3 "마력 강화", white pixel arcane star inside muted purple diamond, description "아군의 마법 피해가 증가합니다.", bonus "마법 피해 +10%", footer "현재 레벨 0".
All three options are equally weighted and selectable; NOT a shop or reward purchase. This is concept-only placeholder content. No currency, cost, reroll, skip, confirm or close button added; no loot or new systems. The entire card is the selection target. No extra small text. Readable Korean block pixel typography with clear title/body/value hierarchy; generous internal padding, all content stays inside frames, icons visually centered. Decorative details mirror cleanly. Keep the image dark and readable, no flat bright backgrounds, no blur/3D/photo, no muddy smearing, no watermark. Output a single finished game screenshot-style concept, not design documentation.
VARIANT 01 — Gothic Metal. Closest to the Figma silhouette: three tall narrow blackened-metal pennants, large diamond emblems overlapping their tops, thin ivory double line edges, muted crimson edging, tapered pointed bottom ends with very restrained chain details. A compact crest and header at top center. Labels occupy matched horizontal baselines. Three equal wide-gapped pennants fill the middle 75% of the screen. Tiny unobtrusive '01' at top-left.
```
